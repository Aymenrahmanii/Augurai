# Eight new models, led by cheap ERNIE 5.5 Flash and Quasar 1.2, and a bare-metal LLM in assembly

## What changed

- **New models (8):** ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview costs $0.28 / $1.13 per million tokens and scores 31.3 on intelligence. Quasar 1.2 358B is cheaper still ($0.11 / $0.33) but scores 23.1. Ling 3.2 Flash has a 1M-token context at $0.30 / $0.90 but no scores yet. Also new: three GigaChat4 variants, Inkling-2-Mini GGUF and CCORE-Web-Coder-4B, which have only Hugging Face trending numbers.
- **Benchmarks:** Reflection's Beam 1.0 entered the speed category (about 392 tokens/s); Muse Ember (Xhigh) left it.
- **Rising repo:** [alesha-pro/llm.asm](https://github.com/alesha-pro/llm.asm) runs Qwen3-0.6B inference in 12 KB of x86-64 assembly, with no libc.
- **Rising agent:** [nexu-io/opengame](https://github.com/nexu-io/opengame) wraps 20+ coding agents into a game-building workflow.
- **Paper:** [OS-Omni](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.34232) benchmarks computer-using agents across web, mobile and desktop.
- **People promoted:** Developing-Gamer, JimLiu and qianyiai.

## One practice to try today

**Compare settings by cost and output, not by name.** Today's list shows why: Quasar 1.2 costs about 2.5x less than ERNIE 5.5 Flash but scores 8 points lower on intelligence. Run one of your real prompts through two models and judge the outputs and the price together.

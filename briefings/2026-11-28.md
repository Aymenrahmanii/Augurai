## One practice to try today

**Dogfood your API by having an AI build against it.** Take a hook or function you designed but never used. First, without AI, spend 10 minutes writing the smallest consumer of it from the docs alone, and note each guess you had to make. Then compare that list with what an AI builder trips over. Source: [datasette-charts 0.4a0](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/27/datasette-charts/).

## One practice to try today

Compare settings by cost and output, not by name. Take [Simon Willison's reasoning-level comparison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/20/llm-gemini/) as the model. First predict by hand which setting will do better and how many tokens it will use. Then run the same prompt at two settings and record tokens, cost and result.

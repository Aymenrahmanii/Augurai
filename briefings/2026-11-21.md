## What changed

- **Benchmark moves:** Claude Fable 5.2 entered the economics, engineering, finance, healthcare, legal and strategy lists. Gemini 4 Neon and GPT-6.2 Astra also entered several categories. Haiku 5.5 entered cost efficiency.
- **Speed and cost:** Celeris-1 entered the speed list at 736 output tokens per second, priced 0.2 in / 0.7 out. Agnes 2.5 Pro Theta entered cost efficiency at 0.1 / 0.3.
- **Caution:** most models were listed as new today (many are older ones just not assessed before), so treat that list as a catch-up, not a launch wave. Several have no scores yet.

## One practice to try today

Before trusting a score in today's benchmark moves, write down what the benchmark measures and one way it could mislead you. Do it by hand first, then read [Eugene Yan's post](https://eugeneyan.com/writing/public-evals) and compare your questions with his.

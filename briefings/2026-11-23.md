## One practice to try today

**Generate a specimen sheet of every option, then choose by eye.** Preferences about visual details are unreliable in the abstract. Seeing every variant side by side in the real browsers makes the choice quick, and it stays your choice. Source: [Select Styling Specimens](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/22/select-styling/).

First try it by hand: pick one UI element you dislike, write four variants on one page, and view them in two browsers. Do this before asking an AI tool to produce the options.

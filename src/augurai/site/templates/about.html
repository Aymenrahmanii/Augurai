About

Augurai watches the AI field for one engineer: new models, benchmarks, projects, people and jobs. It refreshes every six hours, finds new people and projects on its own, and writes a short briefing each morning.

How it works

  1. Every six hours it reads public sources and ranks what moved.
  2. Once a day, Claude summarises what is new and extracts practices from the people's work.
  3. If the summary step fails, the site and email still ship with the ranked lists.

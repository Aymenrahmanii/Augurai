About

Augurai watches the AI field for one engineer: new models, benchmarks, projects, people and jobs. It refreshes every six hours, finds new people and projects on its own, and writes a short briefing each morning.

How it works

  1. Every six hours it reads public sources and ranks what moved.
  2. Once a day, Claude summarises what is new and extracts practices from the people's work.
  3. If the summary step fails, the site still ships with the ranked lists.

Learning with AI, not through it

Augurai is built so you understand more, not so an agent does more of your thinking. These habits keep it that way:

  1. Predict first. Before you read an answer or run a tool, write down what you expect. The gap between your guess and the result is where you learn.
  2. Try it by hand once. Every practice in the Playbook has an exercise. Do it without an AI the first time, then use AI to go faster at something you understand.
  3. Ask for the why. Ask the agent to explain its reasoning and what it rejected. If you could not explain the change to a colleague, you are not done.
  4. Make the agent show its work. Ask for a small diff or one command at a time, and read it before you accept it.
  5. Explain it back. Finish by writing two sentences in your own words. If you cannot, ask again.

The weekly coach follows the same rules: it explains why, gives you something to try first, and only then shows the change. A ready-made CLAUDE.md snippet is in docs/LEARNING.md.
